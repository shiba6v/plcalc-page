Before you start

Forgotten PINs and passwords cannot be recovered. Keep separate backups of important files. Storage depends on available device space.

Wi-Fi transfers use unencrypted HTTP. Use a trusted network: devices on the same LAN can access the shared album while transfer is enabled. OS limits can interrupt background transfers.
